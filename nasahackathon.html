NASA SpaceApps Challenge 2023
We were a team of students that created a data-driven solution to detect and forecast wildfires using satellite imagery.

Know more

About the Challenge

In October 2023, our team took part in the NASA Space Apps Challenge, the world's largest global hackathon, aimed at using open data from NASA and its partners to tackle real world problems. We chose to address the theme of wildfires, one of the most devastating and frequent natural disasters.

Our project, titled "Fighting Fire with Fire", focused on predicting and identifying wildfires using a combination of satellite data, thermal imaging, and AI-based classification. The core goal was to enable early detection and risk mapping for disaster response teams.

The team

We formed a cross disciplinary team of five students from mechanical, electronic, and computer engineering backgrounds. Despite having different skill sets, we collaborated intensively over 48 hours, handling everything from frontend development to a physical prototype.

Our SOlution

We created a platform that leverages MODIS satellite thermal data and NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System) to:

Detect active fires

Forecast high risk zones using AI and weather conditions

Visualize real-time hotspots on an interactive map

Alert relevant authorities or users through simple notifications

My Contribution

As a mechanical engineering student, I helped the team in several areas:

I designed and 3D printed a functional prototype of the hardware device we envisioned for early forest fire detection. This set our project apart, as we were one of the few teams to present a physical prototype. This was an uncommon move in a software focused hackathon. It became a key part of our pitch and contributed significantly to the success of our solution.

I helped source and pre-process MODIS data, ensuring it was usable for our model.

Design + Presentation: I helped storyboard our final pitch and ensured we told a compelling story for our judges and audience.

It was one of the first times I got to work in a team and got a good glimpse into data science and satellite applications. From this I learned a huge amount.

Impact & Result

Our project was praised by local mentors for its technical ambition and relevance in an era of climate change. We were proud to be awarded 1st place in the Ireland regional competition, which took place in Athlone, standing out for both our technical execution and the rare inclusion of a physical prototype. This win qualified us to advance to the global judging stage, where our solution was evaluated alongside some of the most innovative projects from around the world.
