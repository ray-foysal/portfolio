About the Team

The UCD UAV team was founded early in 2024 and recruiting had just begun. I joined the team in February of 2024...

My role on the team spanned from simulations to aeromechanical and manufacturing.

My Role on the Team

The UCD UAV team was created in order to compete in the iMecheE UAS Challenge. The goal was to develop a fully autonomous humanitarian aid drone capable of precise mission planning, payload delivery and safe recovery while complying with the competition regulations.
