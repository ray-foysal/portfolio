About me

My name is Ray Foysal and I'm a 3rd year mechanical engineering student studying in University College Dublin, but currently on exchange at the Australian National University. I'm passionate about bringing cool ideas to life through design and technology.

I've worked on many projects from building race cars for the track to launching UAVs in the sky, but in the middle of everything that I do is my goal: to help people, especially those most vulnerable, and to make the world better than the way I found it, even if its just a little. Whether its designing technology that solves problems, supporting the people around me, or building tools to empower others, I want my work to be part of a positive change, because I believe that even the smallest impact can ripple outwards.

Projects

UCD UAV

The UCD UAV team was created in order to compete in the iMecheE UAS Challenge. The goal was to develop a fully autonomous humanitarian aid drone capable of precise mission planning, payload delivery and safe recovery while complying with the competition regulations.

Formula Students

The UCD Formula Students is a team of engineering students building a fully electric race car from the ground up to compete in the iMechE Formula Student UK competition. It is an international competition that takes place in Silverstone every year.

Find Out More

RoboWars

The goal of the RoboWars projects was to compete in the RoboWars Fight Night 2025 which took place in Dogpatch, Dublin. The robot we created featured a heavy-duty pneumatic piston connected to a flipper arm used to launch other robots into the air.

Find Out More

Robotic Chess Arm

The robotic chess arm was developed as part of a project to create a system that allowed you to play against the computer in chess. The robotic arm featured a dual SCARA arm mechanism that allowed movement in 3-axis.

Find Out More

NASA SpaceApps

Our team won 1st place in the Ireland national round of the 2023 NASA Space Apps Challenge with a system designed to detect wildfires early using satellite data. I designed and 3D printed a working prototype, one of the few hardware implementations presented in the competition.

Find Out More

Podcast on AI and reflective Assignments

A student-led podcast series by UCD exploring how AI is reshaping education and assessment. I produced Episode 5, which focused on the impact of generative AI on reflective assignments, overseeing planning, studio setup, and script editing.

Find Out More

Micromouse

An autonomous maze solving robot built to compete in a Micromouse challenge. I was responsible for the full mechanical design and assembly of the bot, working with motors, safety switches, and supporting electronics integration.
